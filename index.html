About Me

I am Siwat Teimtad, an economist dedicated to clarifying economic theory, market behavior, and strategic decision structures. My work bridges foundational economic philosophy with rigorous formal models to uncover how incentives truly operate in real-world systems.

Whether analyzing the unseen secondary consequences of fiscal policy or modeling competitive interactions through game-theoretic frameworks, my philosophy centers on clarity, intellectual honesty, and first-principles thinking: Master Economics, the Simple Way.

My primary research interests include Classical & Austrian Economics, Microeconomic Market Design, Strategic Form Game Theory, and Incentive Architectures in Modern Digital Economies.
